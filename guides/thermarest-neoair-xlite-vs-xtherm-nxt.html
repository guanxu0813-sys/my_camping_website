Evidence and decision points

  • Weight: representative catalog weights are about 0.33 kg for XLite NXT and 0.44 kg for XTherm NXT.
  • Positioning: Therm-a-Rest calls XLite an all-season do-it-all pad and XTherm its warmth-to-weight specialist.
  • Price: XTherm NXT currently starts about $50 above XLite NXT.
  • Shape: compare these standard models separately from the wider rectangular MAX variants.
  • Decision: choose by expected overnight temperatures first, then compare the 110 g representative weight difference.

Method and limitations

This is a specification comparison, not a hands-on review. Weights and prices can vary by size, fabric, season and market. We use representative values from brand listings and link the source so you can verify the exact variant before buying.
