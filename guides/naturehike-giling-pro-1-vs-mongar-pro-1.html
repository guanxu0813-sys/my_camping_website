Naturehike solo tent comparison

Naturehike Giling Pro 1 vs Mongar Pro 1

These solo tents share the same current reference price but serve different use cases. Naturehike positions the Giling Pro for four-season protection and the lighter Mongar Pro for three-season trekking.

Open full tents table

Bottom line

Choose the Giling Pro 1 for its four-season positioning, semi-solid inner and reinforced quick-pitch X-frame. Choose the Mongar Pro 1 for three-season trips when its roughly 230 g lower listed weight and freestanding double-wall layout are the better fit. Both currently list at $149.

Evidence and decision points

  • Weight: the Mongar Pro is listed at about 1.97 kg, roughly 230 g lighter than the 2.2 kg Giling Pro.
  • Season positioning: Naturehike markets the Giling Pro as a four-season model and the Mongar Pro for spring-through-fall use.
  • Structure: the Giling Pro emphasizes an integrated quick-pitch X-frame; the Mongar Pro uses a freestanding double-wall layout.
  • Price: both records currently carry the same $149 official reference price.
  • Decision: choose from expected weather and structure first; the listed weight difference is secondary to the intended season.

Method and limitations

This is a specification comparison, not a storm-worthiness test. Season labels describe Naturehike's intended positioning and do not guarantee performance in every winter or alpine condition. Listed weights and the $149 reference prices can change by variant or market; verify the linked official listings before purchase.
