How to choose

  • Temperature label: the SP700 model is listed at 19°F / -7.3°C; the SP550 at 29.3°F / -1.5°C.
  • Weight: representative catalog weights are about 1.5 kg for the SP700 and 1.2 kg for the SP550.
  • Insulation: both listings describe 650-fill-power duck down, but the model names represent different fill amounts and warmth targets.
  • Price: the SP550 starts lower; choose by the exact size and temperature requirement rather than model number alone.
  • Temperature warning: brand model labels are not interchangeable with independently tested ISO comfort ratings.

Method and limitations

This is a specification comparison, not a hands-on review. Weights and prices can vary by size, fabric, season and market. We use representative values from brand listings and link the source so you can verify the exact variant before buying.
