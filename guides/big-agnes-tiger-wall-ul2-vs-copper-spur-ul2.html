Big Agnes Tiger Wall UL2 vs Copper Spur UL2

These two-person, three-season Big Agnes tents trade about 195 g of representative catalog weight against pitch structure and features. Tiger Wall UL2 is semi-freestanding and lighter; Copper Spur UL2 is fully freestanding and adds an awning-style door plus more storage features.

Compare these 2 models Open full tents table

Bottom line

Choose Tiger Wall UL2 when saving about 195 g matters more than having a fully freestanding pitch. Choose Copper Spur UL2 when freestanding structure, awning-style doors and additional storage features are worth about $100 and 195 g more. Both footprints are sold separately, and neither specification set proves better storm performance or two-person comfort for your trip.

Choose in three steps

  1. Need the lower representative weight and can stake the foot end for a full pitch? Start with Tiger Wall UL2.
  2. Need a fully freestanding platform or value awning-style doors and additional storage features? Start with Copper Spur UL2.
  3. Before buying, confirm that two-person floor space, packed size and regional availability fit your actual trip; neither model includes its footprint.

Evidence and decision points

  • Representative catalog weight: the current official product records expose about 0.993 kg for Tiger Wall UL2 and 1.188 kg for Copper Spur UL2, a difference of roughly 195 g. These storefront variant weights are used consistently here; they are not relabeled as trail or packed weight.
  • Pitch architecture: Big Agnes describes Tiger Wall UL2 as semi-freestanding with a single-pole architecture. Copper Spur UL2 is fully freestanding and does not require staking the corners for a full pitch.
  • Current US reference price: Tiger Wall UL2 is $499.95 and Copper Spur UL2 is $599.95, a $100 difference on the official US pages checked November 3, 2026.
  • Shared baseline: both are two-person, three-season, dual-door tents using HyperBead fabric. Both official pages say the footprint is sold separately.
  • Feature trade-off: Copper Spur lists awning-style doors and additional storage features; Tiger Wall emphasizes a lighter minimalist side-entry design. These descriptions do not establish a weather-resistance winner.

When the obvious choice can be wrong

  • Tiger Wall UL2 is not automatically the better backpacking choice: a campsite or user who values a fully freestanding pitch may reasonably accept the extra weight of Copper Spur UL2.
  • Copper Spur UL2 is not automatically more comfortable for every pair: listed capacity and feature count do not prove that its floor area or headroom fits two people and their equipment.

Method and limitations

Method: CampGear Compare manually checked both current Big Agnes US product pages and their official Shopify product records on 2026-11-03. The comparison uses the current storefront variant weights (993 g and 1,188 g), US prices, manufacturer architecture labels and listed features. It is a specification comparison, not a hands-on test of setup, space, condensation, durability or weather resistance. Capacity labels do not prove comfort for two people, and product weight may differ from trail, packed or minimum weight definitions used elsewhere. Prices, colors and availability can change; both footprints are sold separately.
