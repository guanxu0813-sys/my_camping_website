How to choose

  • Weight: Tiger Wall UL2 is listed around 0.99 kg versus 1.19 kg for Copper Spur UL2.
  • Architecture: Tiger Wall uses a lightweight single-pole architecture; Copper Spur is described as fully freestanding.
  • Price: Tiger Wall UL2 currently starts about $100 below Copper Spur UL2.
  • Shared material: both current listings highlight Big Agnes HyperBead fabric.
  • Decision: prioritize Tiger Wall for the lower representative weight; prioritize Copper Spur when freestanding structure and interior comfort matter more.
