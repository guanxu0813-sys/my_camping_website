Solo trekking-pole tent comparison

Lanshan 1 vs Lanshan 1 Pro

The standard Lanshan 1 and Lanshan 1 Pro target the same solo ultralight buyer, but they make a different trade-off between a double-wall layout and lower packed weight.

Open full tents table

Bottom line

Choose the standard Lanshan 1 if you want a separate inner and fly, factory-taped seams and a more familiar way to manage condensation. Choose the Lanshan 1 Pro if saving roughly 230 g matters more and you are comfortable managing a single-wall shelter and sealing its silicone-coated seams before wet-weather use.

Choose in three steps

  1. Need a removable inner, factory-taped seams or more separation from condensation? Start with the standard Lanshan 1.
  2. Prioritizing the lowest shelter weight and comfortable maintaining a single-wall tent? Shortlist the Lanshan 1 Pro.
  3. Before buying, match the season inner or fabric variant and compare total carried weight with stakes, guylines and storage sack included.

Evidence and decision points

  • Weight: the Pro starts about 230 g lighter, but its 0.69-0.84 kg range depends on the selected setup.
  • Construction: the standard model separates inner and fly; the Pro combines them in a lighter single-wall layout.
  • Wet-weather preparation: the standard listing specifies taped seams, while 3F UL Gear sells seam sealing as a separate service for Lanshan Pro tents.
  • Season choice: the standard listing offers mesh and solid-inner options; verify the selected variant instead of relying on the model name alone.
  • Price: compare the tent-only variant because current official price ranges can include a footprint or accessory option.

When the obvious choice can be wrong

  • The Pro is not automatically the better ultralight choice: a humid-climate beginner may get more practical value from the heavier double-wall model.
  • The standard model is not automatically the safer choice: in dry, well-ventilated conditions, its separate inner may add weight without solving the buyer's main constraint.

Method and limitations

This is a specification comparison, not a hands-on weather test. The representative 0.92 kg standard weight and 0.69-0.84 kg Pro range use different setup and packing bases, so compare the exact variant and included components. Current official prices can include footprints or accessories. The Pro's seam-sealing requirement and single-wall condensation trade-off do not predict performance in every climate.
