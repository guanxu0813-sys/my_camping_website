Naturehike Mongar Base capacity comparison

Naturehike Mongar Base 2 vs Base 3

Mongar Base 2 and Base 3 use the same listed 210T polyester fabric, PU 3000 mm waterproof rating, aluminum pole material and core accessory kit. The useful decision is whether a lower carried load matters more than one additional listed sleeping place and extra room.

Open full tents table

Bottom line

Choose Mongar Base 2 for solo use or two campers who prioritize the lower load and $30 lower reviewed reference price. Choose Base 3 for three listed sleeping places, or for two campers willing to carry about 0.52 kg more minimum weight and 0.65 kg more total weight for additional room. Capacity labels do not prove comfort for three adults plus equipment.

Choose in three steps

  1. Only one or two people, and carried weight is the main constraint? Start with Base 2.
  2. Need three listed sleeping places, or more room for two people and equipment? Consider Base 3.
  3. Before buying, verify the current regional price and compare minimum weight separately from total weight with accessories.

Evidence and decision points

  • Minimum weight: Base 2 is listed at approximately 2.33 kg and Base 3 at approximately 2.85 kg, a difference of about 0.52 kg.
  • Total weight: with the listed accessory kit, Base 2 is approximately 2.75 kg and Base 3 approximately 3.4 kg, a difference of about 0.65 kg.
  • Capacity: the official labels are two people and three people. They are planning limits, not evidence of comfort with three adults and their gear.
  • Shared specification: both official listings describe 210T ripstop polyester with PU 3000 mm waterproofing, Yuksom aluminum alloy poles and the same core kit.
  • Reviewed price: the US reference prices observed were $129 for Base 2 and $159 for Base 3. Discounts and regional availability can change.
  • Decision: Base 2 saves weight and cost; Base 3 buys one additional listed place and more usable room.

When the obvious choice can be wrong

  • Base 3 is not automatically better for two people: the extra room may not justify carrying another 0.65 kg with the full listed kit.
  • Base 2 is not automatically the better backpacking choice: two campers with bulky equipment may value Base 3 space more than the weight saving.
