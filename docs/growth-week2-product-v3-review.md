# Week 2 — Product-visible promotion loop v3

## Corrected quality gate

The prior creatives failed because the website itself was not the product on screen. V3 is accepted only when a viewer can see a real CampGear Compare interface: search, table, selected comparison, product detail, report, or guide evidence.

## Loop record

1. Capture: opened the local website at desktop size and captured the real report, catalog search, two-item selection, product modal, and guide comparison tables.
2. Build: placed a full browser view plus a magnified interaction crop in every Pinterest creative.
3. Animate: built each Short as a moving walkthrough of two or three real website states. The opening three seconds show both a hook and the working product.
4. Review: inspect all 15 pins at mobile contact-sheet size; inspect four frames from every Short; decode every video end to end.
5. Gate: reject any asset without visible site UI, a single decision, continuous visual change, an explicit conclusion, or an independent UTM.

## Audience review

- Camper: the material demonstrates what happens after the click and makes the decision workflow concrete.
- Content marketer: the creative and landing experience now match; test variables isolate headline/UI crop for pins and hook/walkthrough order for Shorts.

Publishing metadata and UTMs are in `docs/week2-product-v3-publishing.csv`.
