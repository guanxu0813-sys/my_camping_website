## Executive Result

The SOP improved the site's technical reliability, comparison paths, source discipline, and decision content. It did not establish that traffic, qualified use, or referring domains increased because the local GA4, GSC, and link data still stop before this SOP began.

## Keep

1. **Technical regression gates.** Continue analytics, comparison-funnel, generated-site, structured-data, RSS, and internal-link checks on every relevant change.
2. **Source-led corrections.** Keep correcting explicit official facts and recording method, date, and limitations.
3. **Naturehike and Big Agnes decision paths.** Preserve the current cluster links and focused decision pages while awaiting fresh query/page evidence.
4. **Small, pre-registered experiments.** Keep frozen baselines, earliest decision dates, minimum samples, and guardrails.
5. **Fail-closed publishing.** Keep drafts out of sitemap/RSS when source checks fail or data is stale.

## Stop

1. Broad product or guide expansion without a current query cluster that passes the click/impression gate.
2. New Pinterest or YouTube asset production until one qualified, attributable site action is verified.
3. The winter sleeping-bag asset until all included rows are refreshed or the asset is reduced to a fully verified subset.
4. Bulk title/description rollouts; earlier cohorts remain sample-insufficient.
5. Page deletion, merging, or `noindex` expansion without current GSC page and link evidence.
6. Affiliate CTA activation until account approval and the traffic/Modal threshold are both documented.

## Expand

No traffic, content, social, outreach, or monetization tactic currently meets the evidence threshold for expansion.

The only practices approved to expand are quality controls rather than acquisition claims:

- Apply the existing source-review and regression-test workflow to future high-value changes.
- Add field-performance measurement before attempting the category payload refactor identified on Day 27.
- Reuse the current UTM naming rules when a single channel test is explicitly resumed.
