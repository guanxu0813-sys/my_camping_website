## Editorial Limits

- This is a specification guide, not a hands-on durability or weather review.
- Stored weights are representative normalized catalog values and are not labeled as packed, trail or minimum weights.
- Prices and availability can change after the review date.
- Older Copper Spur HV records, mtnGLO variants and larger UL4/UL5 models are outside this guide's scope.
