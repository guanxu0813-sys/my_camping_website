## User question

**Should a solo camper or pair choose the Mongar Base 2, or accept the extra
weight and cost of the Base 3 for more sleeping and gear space?**

The article must make a capacity decision. It must not present a generic Mongar
family catalog or claim hands-on weather performance.

## Proposed comparison scope

- Mongar Base 2-Person Backpacking Tent
- Mongar Base 3-Person Backpacking Tent
- Compare capacity, inner and outer dimensions, packed size, minimum weight,
  total weight with accessories, current reference price, included items,
  fabric, waterproof rating, doors/ventilation and expandable awning.
- Exclude Mongar UL and Mongar Pro models; they use different positioning and
  would blur the Base capacity decision.
- Exclude durability, storm resistance and comfort claims not supported by a
  controlled test.

## Tentative editorial conclusion

- Base 2 is the likely choice for one or two occupants prioritizing lower
  carried weight and lower cost.
- Base 3 is the likely choice for two occupants wanting more living/gear room,
  or for three occupants who accept the added load.
- Based on current technical copy, Base 3 adds about 0.52 kg minimum or 0.65 kg
  total. Prices and sale states are volatile and must be timestamped rather
  than treated as permanent savings.

This conclusion is provisional until Day 26 confirms dimensions, weight labels
and regional price presentation on both official pages.
