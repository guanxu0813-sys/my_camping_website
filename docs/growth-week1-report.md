## Executive Summary

Search visibility expanded strongly during the first complete measurement week. GSC clicks increased from 9 to 23 and impressions increased from 453 to 1,037 against the immediately preceding seven days. GA4 recorded 26 sessions, including 9 organic sessions, and 184 events.
