# Day 29 Data Report and Editorial Outreach

Execution date: 2026-08-12 to 2026-08-13

## Report improvement

The generated 2026 weight and price report now includes three reproducible,
cross-category findings:

1. 64.5% of sleeping pads and 46.6% of sleeping bags are 1 kg or less, versus
   11.7% of tents in the cleaned sample.
2. 86.7% of USD-priced sleeping pads are listed at $200 or less, versus 42.9%
   of USD-priced tents.
3. The tarp median is 4.94 kg versus 2.65 kg for tents because this catalog's
   tarp category includes large shelter systems as well as backpacking tarps.

The report labels these as descriptive catalog findings rather than market
shares. All price percentages remain restricted to USD records.

## Outreach standard

- Contact only editors whose publication has current camping, backpacking,
  outdoor gear or gear-price coverage.
- Put one relevant result in each note and link to the report and CSV.
- Disclose that the sender built CampGear Compare and offer the data for
  inspection, not a paid placement or reciprocal link.
- Do not follow up unless the editor replies or the later outreach review
  explicitly allows one follow-up.
