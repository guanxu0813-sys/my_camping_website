## Editorial Guardrails

- Model numbers are treated as temperature tiers, not capacities.
- The comfort figure is presented as the more conservative first planning point; lower limit is not described as a routine comfort target.
- Regular and Long share published temperature ratings. Long changes fit, weight and price.
- Montbell UK labels the figures as ISO tested, but CampGear Compare has not independently reviewed laboratory reports or performed thermal testing.
- Personal warmth depends on the complete sleep system and conditions, so the guide does not guarantee comfort at a listed temperature.
- The Regular #3 product was sold out when reviewed; its listed price is retained only as an official reference price.
