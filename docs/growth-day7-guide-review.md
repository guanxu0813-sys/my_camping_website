## Editorial Guardrails

- Capacity numbers describe sleeping space and do not prove stronger weather performance, comfort or quality.
- Tiger Wall is described by Big Agnes as a three-season, semi-freestanding family.
- The Platinum name is not treated as an automatic weight win: the representative UL2 and Tiger Wall 2 Platinum records both normalize to about 0.99 kg.
- Bikepack models are recommended for their 12-inch pole sets, mounting sacks and bike storage features, not as general upgrades.
- The UL3 Bikepack Solution Dye discount is temporary and is not used to infer current Bikepack pricing.
- Representative catalog weights are not labeled as interchangeable packed, trail or minimum weights.
