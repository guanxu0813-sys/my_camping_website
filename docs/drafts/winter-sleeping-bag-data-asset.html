Method and limitations

  1. Read the existing official-product catalog snapshot and keep sleeping bags with an explicit Celsius comfort value.
  2. Reject Fahrenheit/Celsius pairs differing by more than 2°C, records warmer than 5°C, and records missing positive weight, price, or official source URL.
  3. Keep USD, GBP, and JPY records separate. No exchange-rate conversion or cross-currency price ranking is performed.
  4. Plot listed comfort temperature against representative catalog weight; preserve the original temperature label in the table.
