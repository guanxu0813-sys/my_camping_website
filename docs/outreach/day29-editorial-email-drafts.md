## Camping Magazine

To: `editorial@campingmagazine.co.uk`

Subject: `Camping gear data: the $200 divide between tents and sleeping pads`

Body:

Hello Camping Magazine editorial team,

I run CampGear Compare and recently cleaned a downloadable dataset of 543
camping products sourced from official brand pages.

For price-focused camping coverage, 42.9% of the 233 USD-priced tents in the
sample are listed at $200 or less. Sleeping pads look very different: 86.7% of
90 USD-priced pads fall at or below that line.

Report: https://www.campgearcompare.com/guides/camping-gear-weight-price-report-2026.html?utm_source=campingmagazine&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=camping-gear-weight-price-2026&utm_content=data_note

are never combined without conversion. Please feel free to inspect or cite the
