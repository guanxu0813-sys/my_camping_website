## Existing updates versus new content

The comparison uses the complete M1 GSC page export. It is descriptive, not a
causal experiment, because pages differ in age, demand and index history.

| Content group | Pages | Clicks | Impressions | CTR |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Substantively updated guides | 5 | 18 | 384 | 4.69% |
| New Mongar Base 2 vs 3 guide | 1 | 3 | 57 | 5.26% |

The updated group contains Tiger Wall models, Giling Pro vs Mongar Pro,
Lanshan 1 vs Pro, Montbell Down Hugger 650 and the weight-price report. Existing
updates produced more absolute search reach. The new Mongar guide has a useful
early CTR and position 5.65, but 57 impressions are too few to justify a second
wave of new pages by itself.

## Retired routine

Recurring manual reopening of closed Reddit threads and repeated inbox searches
for old outreach are removed from the M2 calendar. All 13 logged items already
say to act only on a direct reply. M2 will react to notifications and use the
saved time for source checks and winner-page updates.

The generic weekend requirement to send up to three new emails every week is
also paused for one month. A new message is allowed only when a named recipient
has a direct fit with a newly improved asset; volume is not a completion metric.
